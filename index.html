OUR PURPOSE01 — 03

A more considered
kind of computing.

The Conscious Systems & Applied AI Lab advances practical software engineering and ethical AI through cognitive resilience, intentional technology, and human-centered design.

Our focus is computer science education research for learners in pre-kindergarten through grade 12. We bring hands-on computing together with reflection, well-being, and questions about how technology shapes the way we learn and live.

HOW WE THINK03 — 03

A practice of making and noticing

Learn to build.
Pause to notice.

Our public language speaks to the practical priorities of schools, research partners, and technology sponsors. Underneath it is a simple belief: education should help people become capable creators, attentive thinkers, and responsible participants in a technological world.

That means learning by doing, asking better questions, and making room for inner awareness and contemplative inquiry—without losing sight of rigorous computer science.
